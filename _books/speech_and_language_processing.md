## Thoughts before Starting

This textbook was recommended by my Machine Learning professor, an expert in NLP and translation. I plan to read the first part in preparation for **CSE244B: Machine Learning for Natural Language Processing**, which I will be taking this coming Winter. I already have a basic understanding of neural language models from **CSE-142: Machine Learning** last Fall, and I’m looking forward to developing a much deeper understanding.
