"whatever gets measured gets done"

Points To Cover:
  You need to have priorities (take pts from -Alex Hormozi video), you need to plan, if you fail to plan you plan to fail. Alex says the word Priorities is wrong!
  You can only have one priority.

    1. 10hrs of dedicated work on my project
    2. Gym + hitting macros
    3. Daily Leetcode
    4. Daily Chess

    Tate Said missing a single day will not affect you achieving your goals but the midset that lets you miss a day will.

    Today is August 1st, for the next month(approx 4 weeks) I want ot go all out, not miss a single day, doesn't matter if I feel like it or not, "Do the hardwork especially when you don't feel like it" -Hamza. 

    Work the hardest when no one is looking, so that you know you are not full of shit.-Hormozi
